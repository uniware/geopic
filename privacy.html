Geopic privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Geopic shows the photos in your OneDrive on a map. It has no server of its own: it runs entirely in your web browser, and your photos only ever travel between your OneDrive and your browser. Geopic doesn't sell data or show ads. The source code is public at github.com/uniware/geopic.

Your OneDrive

When you sign in with Microsoft, you give Geopic permission to:

  • Read your files. Microsoft's permission covers all your files, but Geopic only reads your Photos folder: each photo's location, date, file name, folder name, tags and thumbnail, and the full photo when you open it.
  • Read and write its own app folder in your OneDrive. Geopic saves its index of your photos there so later visits are fast. It can't change or delete any of your other files.

Your browser also keeps a copy of the index and your sign-in tokens, so you stay signed in. Logging out (the ⏏ button) removes both from your browser.

Google Maps

The map and the place search are provided by Google Maps. Like any website, Google receives your IP address and which parts of the map you view. When you type a place name into the search box, that text goes to Google to suggest places. Your photos and their locations aren't sent to Google: the map draws them in your browser. Google's handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics

Geopic uses Google Analytics to count how many people use it. It records page views, scrolling, when someone signs in, visits while signed in, and finishes indexing, with the number of photos rounded to a power of ten (for example "1000+"). It never records your name, email address, file names, photos or photo locations, and the page address it reports never includes the sign-in details.

Google Analytics sets cookies and, like any website, sees your IP address and your browser and device type, from which it estimates your rough location. To opt out, use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on or a content blocker.

Hosting

Geopic's web pages are hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard request logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) to run and protect its service.

Removing your data

  • From your browser: log out with the ⏏ button.
  • From your OneDrive: delete Geopic's folder inside the Apps folder.
  • Revoke Geopic's access: for a personal Microsoft account, go to account.live.com/consent/Manage; for a work or school account, go to myapplications.microsoft.com.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/uniware/geopic/issues. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
